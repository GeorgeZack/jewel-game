# Attributions

## Art made with AI

The game's pixel art was generated with [Retro Diffusion](https://www.retrodiffusion.ai/)
and then picked, cropped and arranged by hand:

- gems, skulls, coins and stars, and their idle animations (`_res/rd-*.png`, `_res/idle/`)
- item icons (`_res/items/`)
- hex terrain sheets and the parchment (`_res/hex/`)
- panels, buttons and bars (`_res/ui/`)
- the world map picture (`assets/maps/`)

Some of it is placeholder and will be replaced.
